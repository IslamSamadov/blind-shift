How to Play

🎯 Objective

Collect every Quantum Cube hidden across both dimensions, then reach the Exit Door before the Stalker catches you.

🕹️ Controls

W A S Dor↑ ← ↓ →Move SPACEPhase Shift

🌀 Dimensions

Press SPACE to flip between the Red and Blue dimension. Each has its own walls and cubes. The Stalker follows you — but sometimes it stays behind.

👁️ Entities

  • ● You — navigate the darkness
  • ● ● Stalker — hunts you. Cannot be killed
  • ■ Quantum Cube — collect all to unlock exit
  • ▮ Exit Door — escape when all cubes collected

💡 Survival Tips

  • Lure the Stalker into open space in one dimension, then shift — a wall may trap it on the other side.
  • Watch for red eyes glowing in the dark — the Stalker is close.
  • The Stalker occasionally lunges — never stand still.
  • Your flashlight may flicker — keep moving.
